# Add a team

A team belongs to an organisation and is what you enter into competitions. You'll usually create several within one organisation, one for each side you run.

## Before you start

If the organisation is a **school**, set its **gender profile** first (Boys only, Girls only or Co-ed). Teams take their gender from the school, so this must be set before you can add teams.

## Steps

1. Open the organisation the team belongs to.
2. Go to the **Teams** section.
3. Click **Add team**.
4. For a **club or association**, pick the **division** (Men's, Ladies, Masters, Open, Boys, Girls). A co-ed school picks **Boys** or **Girls**; single-gender schools skip this.
5. Choose the **level**:
   - **Senior** — an ordinal from 1st Team to 10th Team, or
   - **Age group** — an age (U19 down to U8; clubs and associations may also use U21) plus a letter (A to J).
6. Check the preview, then click **Add team**.

The name is composed for you (for example, "Riverside College U16A"). The team now appears under your organisation and is ready to be entered into a competition.

## Adding several teams

If you run multiple sides, repeat the steps for each. Because the name is built from your choices, the format stays consistent across all of them automatically.
